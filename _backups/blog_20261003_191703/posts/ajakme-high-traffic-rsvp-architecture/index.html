01. Background & Spiky Concurrency Problem

AjakMe is an end-to-end digital wedding planner and invitation SaaS platform. Traffic is heavily burst-oriented: on weekends, thousands of guests access invitations simultaneously to view venues, submit RSVPs, and sign digital guestbooks within narrow time windows.

03. Architectural Solutions

Worker Queue Isolation

Heavy operations like WhatsApp/email dispatch are assigned to dedicated secondary queues. Guest RSVP inserts remain entirely unblocked on the primary priority pipeline, preventing queue starvation.

Atomic Redis In-Memory Counters

Live headcount counts increment in Redis atomically and flush lazily to MySQL in batches, reducing concurrent write lock pressure on SQL tables during event peaks.
