01. Executive Overview

Architected and led the development of ACCEA's centralized identity and shared services platform. Unifies customer authentication across 5+ independent applications (room reservation, customer portals, printing systems) while powering cross-product promotional campaigns with sub-100ms real-time coupon verification.

03. Technical Implementation

Unified Single Sign-On

Consolidated multiple legacy customer database tables into a single source of truth for authentication. Eliminated duplicate login flows across ACCEA services.

High-Throughput Coupon Rule Validation

Engineered an in-memory rule engine caching campaign definitions and eligibility rules in Redis. Leveraged atomic locks to avoid race conditions during peak redemption spikes, reducing database load by 40-60%.
