Upgrading AjakMe Architecture

Published_On 2026-03-28
Architecture_Tags
Infrastructure Refactoring

Migration Goals

The primary objective for the 2.0 release of AjakMe was to transition from a monolithic architecture to a modular substrate that can handle horizontal scaling across multiple regions.

  • Implementing a unified event-driven dispatcher.
  • Refactoring domain logic into isolated service modules.
  • Optimization of database query patterns for high-concurrency environments.

Current Progress

I am currently focused on the decoupled domain logic, ensuring that each module operates with zero-knowledge of its surrounding environment, communicating solely through strict interface protocols.

Challenges

The main bottleneck remains the legacy dataset migration, requiring careful ETL processes to ensure zero-downtime during the primary cutover.
